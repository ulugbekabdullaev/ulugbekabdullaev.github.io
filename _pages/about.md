I am a master's student at [Elmore Family School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Purdue University](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE) working with Prof. [Vijay Gupta](https://engineering.purdue.edu/Vijay-Gupta). I am interested in control theory, game theory and optimization. I am conducting research on differential games, with a particular focus on Target-Attacker-Defender game. My work involves identifying and analyzing optimal strategies for agents to achieve their objectives. I completed my bachelor's degree in Automation and Control from [Tashkent State Transport University](https://tstu.uz/), Uzbekistan, in 2023.
